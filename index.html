Who I Am

Background & Licenses

I am a Licensed Civil Engineer and Licensed Master Plumber based in Mabalacat, Pampanga, Philippines. My career started in structural and plumbing design for local and international building projects, before moving into groundwater engineering and construction dewatering at WJ Groundwater Philippines Corp.

At WJ Groundwater, I handle technical proposals and cost estimates for dewatering and water supply projects across the Middle East, Saudi Arabia, and the Philippines — over 60 submissions to date, including tenders for major infrastructure projects such as the Abu Dhabi–Dubai Etihad Railway and the Cebu–Mactan Bridge.

Beyond engineering, I build VBA-based automation tools to streamline reporting workflows — reducing manual processing time by approximately 60–80% on active projects. I am actively upskilling in Revit to strengthen my BIM capabilities alongside my existing AutoCAD and SEEP/W skill set.

I am open to remote or hybrid roles with international teams, particularly in estimating, technical engineering, and project delivery.
