Who I Am

Background & Licenses

I am a Licensed Civil Engineer and Licensed Master Plumber based in Mabalacat, Pampanga, Philippines. My career started in structural and plumbing design for local and international building projects before moving into groundwater engineering and construction dewatering at WJ Groundwater Philippines Corp.

At WJ Groundwater, I handle technical proposals and cost estimates for dewatering and water supply projects across the Middle East, Saudi Arabia, and the Philippines — over 60 submissions to date. These include tenders for major infrastructure such as the Abu Dhabi–Dubai Etihad Railway and the Cebu–Mactan Bridge.

Outside of core engineering, I build VBA-based automation tools to streamline reporting workflows — reducing manual processing time by approximately 60–80% on active projects. I am also actively upskilling in Revit alongside my existing AutoCAD and SEEP/W capabilities.

I am open to remote or hybrid roles with international engineering firms, particularly in estimating, technical engineering, and project delivery.
