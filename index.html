Who I Am

Background & Licenses

I am a Licensed Civil Engineer and Licensed Master Plumber based in Mabalacat, Pampanga, Philippines. My career began in structural and plumbing design for local and international building projects before transitioning to groundwater engineering and construction dewatering at WJ Groundwater Philippines Corp.

At WJ Groundwater, I handle technical proposals and cost estimates for dewatering and water supply projects across the Middle East, the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, and the Philippines — over 60 submissions to date. These include tenders for major infrastructure such as the Abu Dhabi–Dubai Etihad Railway and the Cebu–Mactan Bridge.

Outside of core engineering, I build VBA-based automation tools to streamline reporting workflows — reducing manual processing time by approximately 60–80%. I am actively upskilling in Revit, Bluebeam, and Primavera P6 to broaden my capabilities in BIM modelling and project scheduling.

I am open to remote or hybrid roles with international engineering firms — particularly in cost estimating, BIM modelling, and technical engineering.
